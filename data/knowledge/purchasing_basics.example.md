# Example Purchasing Checklist (Synthetic)

This is sample content for testing only. Replace it with approved internal procedures stored outside version control.

Before reviewing a purchase order, an authorized buyer may need to verify:
- The request has an approved purchase requisition.
- Supplier identity matches the approved supplier record.
- Item description, quantity, unit of measure, and currency are consistent with the quotation.
- Price, taxes, delivery terms, and expected delivery date are documented.
- Required approvals are recorded according to company policy.

This example is not a real company policy. Always follow the organization's approved procedures.
